heatmap-geometry demos

Live builds of the MapLibre GL JS heatmap-geometry branch.

  • Create a heatmap of lines and polygons: Atlantic storm tracks and wind swaths, comparing vertices and shapes
  • Create a heatmap layer: the point heatmap example, with radius and colour controls

Storm data derived from NOAA National Hurricane Center HURDAT2 and best track archives; not an official NHC product.
